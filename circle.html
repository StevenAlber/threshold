The Invitation

A category does not begin alone.

Every art form that becomes a category — from chamber music in private salons to the modern intimate retreat — was first held in being by a small circle of people who believed in it before the world did. THRESHOLD is at this moment.

The first private THRESHOLD evening will take place in Asia in June 2026. The room, the host, the twenty-five guests, the camera, the documentation, the photography — these together establish what THRESHOLD is, both as a vocal practice and as a category that did not exist before.

We are inviting thirty-one patrons to make this first chapter possible. The number is intentionally small. The relationship is intentionally personal. Each patron receives something real in return — not gratitude, but objects, films, and presence that no public market will ever sell.

On Integrity

A few things you should know.

This is not crowdfunding

THRESHOLD is positioned in the international luxury market. The Founding Circle is not a public donation campaign. It is a closed circle whose members will be remembered in the artist's career as those who held the beginning. There is no progress bar, no public pledge counter, no comments section.

If we cannot fill the circle

If by 31 May 2026 the full €29,000 is not committed, THRESHOLD will still launch — at a smaller scale, with what is gathered. Every patron will still receive their full benefits. Nothing is held hostage to a fundraising threshold.

Documentation and accounting

Every patron receives, after the first Asian evening, a private financial summary of how the budget was spent. This is not investor reporting in the venture-capital sense — it is courtesy between the artist and the people who made the work possible.

The artist does not enter the room to entertain. She enters to change the room.
